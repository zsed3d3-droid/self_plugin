# Local decision policy

This distribution contains no personal judgments or reasons. Review candidate rules with `harness/feedback-review`, then export your own completed journal with `../scripts/sync-decisions.py`. Store the export and its scoped application map in your private project policy directory. Interpret each reason within its stated context; do not invert rejected rules or promote assistant examples into user preferences. Until a policy is explicitly connected, report personal-policy validation as not configured.
